# Center for Climate, Health and Healthcare

We are part of Rutgers University’s Institute for Health, Health Care Policy and
Aging Research. Our work examines how climate change affects health and how
healthcare systems can respond.

## Explore our work

This GitHub organization is where we share public research code, tools, and
documentation from the center.

- [Center website](https://sites.rutgers.edu/center-for-climate-health-and-healthcare/) — Learn about our research and team.
- [Public repositories](https://github.com/RWJB-Rutgers-CCHH/resources/wiki) — Browse
  available code and documentation.
